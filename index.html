01 / THE PROJECT

Support the call.
Keep the human in charge.

Squash rallies happen quickly, and decisions about interference can be difficult to review. This project explores an AI-assisted workflow that observes player movement, flags potentially relevant moments, and gives a trained human official material to review.

Core principle
AI can assist with observation. The human referee remains responsible for the final rules-based decision.
